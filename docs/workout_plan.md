# Workout Plan (frozen 21 Nov 2026)

A 6-day split done **in order** (not tied to weekdays), plus a finisher after every workout and a Full Body backup day. Goal: build muscle, prioritising upper chest, lats and shoulders.

The app seeds its routines from [`app/plan.py`](../app/plan.py). Keep that file in sync with this document.

**C** = compound (several joints, heavier, done first) · **I** = isolation (one joint, done after)

## Rules for every session

- **Warm-up (5 min):** light cardio or band work, then 1–2 lighter sets of the first exercise.
- **Rest:** 2–3 min after compound sets, 60–90 s after isolation sets.
- **Effort:** finish each set with 1–2 reps left in the tank.
- **Progression:** once every set reaches the top of the rep range, add the smallest weight step next time.
- **Same machine every time:** stack numbers differ between machines.
- **Incline bench at about 30°** (15–30° is fine) for incline presses. Steeper than about 45° turns it into a shoulder press.
- **Easier week every 6–8 weeks:** same exercises and weights, about half the sets, shorter finisher.

## Finisher (after every workout, about 15 min)

| # | Exercise | Type | Prescription |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Farmer walk, 20 kg each hand (suitcase carry on 1–2 days a week, optional) | C | 5 rounds × ~45 s, ~60 s rest |
| 2 | Floor crunch | I | 20 slow reps (3 s up, 3 s down). Hold a plate when easy |
| 3 | Superman (face down) | I | 12–15 reps, 1–2 s hold at the top |
| 4 | Side heel touches, or a 30–45 s plank / dead bugs on 2–3 days a week | I | 20 reps |
| 5 | Stretching: cat-cow, full-body yoga stretch | n/a | 5 min |

**Farmer walk progression:** when every round feels easy with an upright posture, go to 22–24 kg per hand, then about 30 kg.

## Backup: Full Body catch-up

Use it as your first session back after missing **2+ days in a row**, then continue the rotation from the day you were due. If you've had 5+ days off, keep 2–3 reps in reserve.

| # | Exercise | Type | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Romanian deadlift | C | 2 × 8–10 |

| 4 | Lat pulldown | C | 3 × 8–12 |
| 5 | Seated cable row | C | 2 × 10–12 |
| 6 | Cable lateral raise | I | 2 × 12–20 |
| 7 | Dumbbell curl, alternated with triceps pushdown | I | 2 × 10–12 each |

- **Compound:** incline dumbbell press, flat dumbbell press, machine chest press, shoulder press, lat pulldown, single-arm cable lat pulldown, chest-supported T-bar row, seated cable row, barbell squat, leg press, hack squat / Bulgarian split squat, Romanian deadlift, hip thrust, farmer walk / suitcase carry.
